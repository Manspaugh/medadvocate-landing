How it works

Three steps. Ninety seconds. One flat fee.

01

Open MedAdvocate and take a picture or upload any medical bill - itemized statement, EOB, collections notice. We accept PDFs and screenshots from your insurance portal too.

  • Take a photo or upload a file, no manual entry
  • Works with paper or digital bills
  • Completely encrypted, never sold
02

We cross-reference your charges against Medicare allowables, fair-price databases, and 600+ known billing-error patterns. You get a line-by-line breakdown in under 90 seconds.

  • Duplicate charges, upcoding, unbundling
  • Pricing vs. fair-market benchmarks
  • Win-rate estimate per error with evidence
03

Select a dispute or negotiation and we generate a formal, evidence-backed letter, and then send it on your behalf through certified mail. One flat fee, no percentage of savings.

  • Backed by FDCPA + state law
  • Certified mail we send for you
  • Average resolution: 14 days
